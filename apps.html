Use another app

Your isotunnel account also works in Hiddify and other apps that take a subscription link, on Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux. Until isotunnel has its own phone app, this is how to use it on your phone.

1. Get Hiddify

It's free. Search for Hiddify in Google Play or the App Store, or download it from its releases on GitHub.

2. Make your link

Open in Hiddify

On your phone, open Hiddify, tap + and choose Scan QR code.

The link is made here in your browser. Your key isn't sent anywhere until an app uses the link.

3. Add it in the app

  1. On this device: press Open in Hiddify. Or copy the link, open Hiddify, tap + and choose Add from clipboard.
  2. On your phone: press Show QR code here, then scan it in Hiddify (+, then Scan QR code).
  3. Pick isotunnel's profile and press connect. Hiddify tests the servers and uses the quickest.

Other apps

Any app that takes a VLESS or Hysteria2 subscription link works the same way: add a subscription and paste the link. For example v2rayN (Windows), v2rayNG (Android), and Streisand or Shadowrocket (iPhone).

Good to know

  • Each app you add it to counts as one of your account's 3 devices.
  • Keep the link private, like your key: anyone who has it can use your account.
  • The app refreshes the server list by itself every 12 hours and shows how much data you've used.
  • Ad blocking, split tunneling and the kill switch are isotunnel app features. In another app, use that app's own settings.
  • Lost your key? Use "Already have a key, or lost it?" on the home page.
