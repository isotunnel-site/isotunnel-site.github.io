What the network sees

From the outside

An encrypted TLS 1.3 connection on port 443 to an ordinary website. The certificate it gets back is that website's real one.

What actually happens

The server recognises your key in that handshake and carries your traffic. Anyone else who connects to it is passed through to the real website.

Through Cloudflare, the network sees an ordinary HTTPS connection to Cloudflare, which serves a large share of the web, and never sees our server's address. The privacy policy says what Cloudflare can see.

Questions

What do you log?

Not the sites you visit, and no logs of your IP address. To run your account we keep your plan, its expiry and how much data you've used. The privacy policy lists everything.

Will it work where I am?

Download the app and press Test this network on the sign-in screen before you buy. It tries every way in from your own network and tells you if isotunnel works there.

Mac or phone?

Windows and Mac now (installing on a Mac). There's no phone app yet.

Will it be fast for me?

It's quickest near a server: Sydney for Australia and New Zealand, Tokyo for Japan. Choose the nearest one with Change location in the app.

Does it cover every app?

Browsers and most apps by default. Turn on full device mode in Settings and every app goes through it, games included. It asks for administrator permission when you connect.

What if I use up my data?

The VPN pauses until your allowance refills next month, or pay for another month for a fresh allowance straight away. The app shows how much you've used.

What if I lose my key?

If the app is still signed in, open Account and press Show next to Access key: you can copy it there. If not, open "Already have a key, or lost it?" in the box where you buy, enter the email you paid with, and we'll send the key there. (Only that address gets it, and only if a card payment used it.) If that doesn't work, email support@isotunnel.com with when you paid. We never see your card number, so we can't look you up by card. Keep your key somewhere safe, like a password manager.

What if it can't connect?

Turn on troubleshooting mode in Settings and send us the report it makes. The sites you visit and your key are left out of it.

What if the app crashes while I'm connected?

Your original settings are put back straight away. The one exception is on purpose: in full device mode with the kill switch on, the internet stays blocked until isotunnel reconnects.
