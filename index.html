How it works

Sign in with your access key and press Connect. The app points Windows' proxy settings at an Xray process, so your browser and most apps send their traffic through our server in Sydney or Tokyo, whichever you choose. Turn on full device mode in Settings and every app goes through it, games included. Press Disconnect and your old settings come back exactly as they were. We don't keep a record of the sites you visit.

The app finds a way in by itself. If the usual way is blocked it tries the others at once, then Cloudflare. To choose one yourself, open Your route in the app, press Edit route and change the way in: Automatic, Port 443 or Cloudflare.

What the network sees

From the outside

Encrypted TLS 1.3 connection on port 443 to an ordinary website. The certificate it gets back is official.

What actually happens

The server recognises the key in that handshake and carries your traffic. Anyone else who connects to it, including a firewall checking up on it, is passed through to the real website.

Through Cloudflare, the network sees an ordinary HTTPS connection to Cloudflare, which serves a large share of the web, and never sees our server's address. The privacy policy says what Cloudflare can see.

Questions

What do you log?

Not the sites you visit, and no logs of your IP address. To run your account we keep your plan, its expiry and how much data you've used. The privacy policy lists everything.

Mac or phone?

Not yet. It's Windows only for now.

Will it be fast for me?

It's quickest near a server: Sydney for Australia and New Zealand, Tokyo for Japan. Choose the nearest one with Change location in the app. From further away your traffic travels to that server first.

Does it cover every app?

Normally it covers apps that use Windows' proxy settings, which includes browsers and most everyday apps. Some games and apps ignore those settings, so for them turn on full device mode in Settings: every app then goes through the VPN. It asks for administrator permission when you connect.

What if I use up my data?

The VPN pauses until your allowance refills next month. Or pay for another month and you get a fresh allowance straight away. The app shows how much you've used.

Will it work where I am?

Download the app and press Test this network on the sign-in screen before you buy. It tries every way in from your own network and tells you if isotunnel works there.

What if my network blocks it?

On Automatic, the app tries every way in, then Cloudflare, without you doing anything. If it still can't connect, turn on troubleshooting mode in Settings and send us the report it makes. The sites you visit and your key are left out of it.

What if the app crashes while I'm connected?

Your original settings are put back straight away, even if it crashes. The one exception is on purpose: in full device mode with the kill switch on, the internet stays blocked until isotunnel reconnects, so nothing goes out unprotected.
